Frequently asked questions

How do I sort photos?

Swipe right to keep a photo, left to mark it for deletion, and up to add it to your Favorites. You can also use the buttons under the photo. Tap undo to take back your last swipe.

Is anything deleted straight away?

No. Photos you swipe away wait in a review list. Tap Review to look them over, restore any you want to keep, then delete the rest. iOS asks you to confirm, and deleted photos go to Recently Deleted in Photos, where you can recover them for 30 days.

How does Similar Photos work?

SnapSkim scans your library on your iPhone and groups near-identical photos, like bursts or several takes of the same shot, into sets. Open a set, pick the photo you want to keep, and mark the rest for deletion in one tap. If it finds too many or too few sets, change the sensitivity in the menu under Similar Photos.

Why can't SnapSkim see all my photos?

If you chose to share only some photos, SnapSkim can only show those. To sort your whole library, go to Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos › SnapSkim on your iPhone and choose Full Access.

What happens when I rotate a photo?

The new orientation is saved to the photo in your library, so it shows the right way up everywhere. Like any edit in Photos, you can undo it later in the Photos app with Edit › Revert.

Can I see the tour again?

Yes. Open the menu in the top-left corner and choose Take the Tour.

Does SnapSkim upload my photos?

Never. Everything happens on your device. There's no account, no sign-in and no data collection, apart from feedback you choose to send. See the privacy policy for details.
