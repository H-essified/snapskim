Privacy Policy

Short version: SnapSkim doesn't collect anything. Your photos stay on your device.

SupportPrivacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026

What SnapSkim collects

Nothing. SnapSkim has no account or sign-in, and it doesn't use analytics, advertising or tracking. It doesn't send your photos, or any information about them, to the developer or to anyone else.

Your photo library

SnapSkim asks for access to your photo library so it can show you your photos and carry out what you ask it to do: keep, favorite, rotate or delete them. All of this happens on your device, including finding similar photos. Photos are only deleted when you confirm it, and iOS moves them to Recently Deleted, where you can recover them for 30 days.

You can change or remove SnapSkim's access at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos.

Data stored on your device

To remember where you got to, SnapSkim stores your settings and a list of which photos you've already sorted. This stays on your device, isn't shared, and is removed when you delete the app.

Sharing

If you use the share button, the photo goes wherever you choose to send it, using the standard iOS share sheet. SnapSkim doesn't see or keep a copy of what you share.

Children

SnapSkim doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
